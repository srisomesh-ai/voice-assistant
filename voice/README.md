# Your voice goes here

- `my_voice.wav` — 10–15 seconds of you speaking (made by `scripts/prepare_voice.py`)
- `my_voice.txt` — the exact words in that clip

These files are ignored by git, so your voice never gets uploaded to GitHub.

Recording tips:
- Quiet room, no fan/AC noise, no music, no echo.
- Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Speak naturally, the way you want the assistant to sound — calm and friendly works best.
- One continuous sentence or two, no long pauses.
- Mixed Telugu + English is fine.
